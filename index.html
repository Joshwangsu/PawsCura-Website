Frequently asked questions

Can PawsCura replace my veterinarian?

No. PawsCura gives AI-assisted informational triage to help you decide how urgently to seek care. It is not a substitute for professional veterinary diagnosis or treatment.

Which pets are supported?

PawsCura currently supports dogs and cats, including multiple pet profiles with breed, birthday, weight and custom card colors.

Is the app really free?

Yes. The Free tier includes 1 pet profile, up to 5 AI scans per month, 5 saved history logs, the 3 nearest clinics and full access to the AI Chatbot. Upgrade to Premium to remove limits and ads.

Why does Android warn me when installing the APK?

Android shows a prompt for any app installed outside the Play Store. Allow “Install from Unknown Sources” for your browser or file manager just for this install, then you can turn it back off.

Is an iOS version coming?

An iOS App Store release is on our future development roadmap. For now PawsCura is available on Android (8.0+).
